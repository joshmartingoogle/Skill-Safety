# Skill-Safety: Assisted vs. Unassisted Skills Empirical Pipeline & Results Brief

This repository contains the directionally standardized **Skill Safety Index Rubric**, the Python scoring and psychometric evaluation pipeline (`pipeline.py` and `analyze_and_plot.py`), the latest occupational task and skill taxonomies (**Stanford TaskNet**, **O\*NET 31.0 Tasks**, and **O\*NET 31.0 Skills**), all statistical and visual artefacts, and the 4-page LaTeX results brief (`skill_safety_results_brief.tex` / `skill_safety_results_brief.pdf`).

## Key Findings Upfront

- **Directional Standardization & Certainty-Weighted Scoring ($N=530$ Items):** All six scored dimensions of the Skill Safety Index (`N1` Fallback, `N2` Live Performance, `N3` No-AI Rules, `N4` Checking, `N5` Foundation, and `S` Stakes) are oriented consistently on a `0`–`3` scale where **higher scores indicate greater skill safety** (lower unassisted need and lower error stakes: `3` = Safest / Assist for Good, `0` = Least Safe / Must Retain Unassisted Ability). Passing every item individually to the Gemini Flash API yields high epistemic certainty ($\bar{c} = 0.903\text{--}0.917$).
- **Bimodal Overall Distributions Across Taxonomies:** Certainty-weighted Skill Safety Index scores ($I_i \in [0,3]$) exhibit a bimodal distribution across **Stanford TaskNet** ($\text{Mean}=1.239$, $\text{SD}=0.896$, $\text{Median}=1.481$, $\text{IQR}=1.811$), **O\*NET 31.0 Tasks** ($\text{Mean}=1.155$, $\text{SD}=0.910$, $\text{Median}=1.209$, $\text{IQR}=1.547$), and **O\*NET 31.0 Skills** ($\text{Mean}=1.323$, $\text{SD}=1.053$, $\text{Median}=1.173$, $\text{IQR}=2.018$). Across taxonomies, $51.0\%\text{--}54.8\%$ of items fall in the low-safety regime ($I_i < 1.5$), while $21.2\%\text{--}34.3\%$ sit in the high-safety regime ($I_i \ge 2.0$).
- **Cross-Industry Ordering & Within-Occupation Boundary:** Across all three taxonomies, **Healthcare: Clinical Medicine (Emergency Medicine Physicians)** exhibits the lowest skill safety ($\text{Mean}=0.175\text{--}0.620$), whereas **Information & Technology (Software Developers)** exhibits the highest skill safety ($\text{Mean}=1.811\text{--}1.903$), followed by **Educational Services** ($1.180\text{--}1.513$) and **Legal & Professional Services** ($1.160\text{--}1.631$). Within Law and Software Development, asynchronous drafting and design tasks score high on fallback/live safety ($N_1, N_2 \approx 2\text{--}3$) but low on foundation and checking safety ($N_4, N_5 \approx 0\text{--}1$), with $96.9\%\text{--}100.0\%$ triggering the learner bypass flag.
- **Scale Reliability (Cronbach's $\alpha$) & Leave-One-Out Diagnostics:** Cronbach's $\alpha_{\text{CW}}$ is **$0.8728$** for Stanford TaskNet, **$0.8764$** for O\*NET 31.0 Tasks, and **$0.9207$** for O\*NET 31.0 Skills (all $> 0.70$). Leave-one-out diagnostics show that **Stakes ($S$)** and **Foundation ($N_5$)** have the lowest item-rest correlations ($r_{\text{rest}} = 0.501\text{--}0.597$); omitting $S$ marginally raises $\alpha_{\text{CW}}$ in TaskNet ($\Delta\alpha = +0.0044$ to $0.8772$) and O\*NET Skills ($\Delta\alpha = +0.0069$ to $0.9277$) because foundational entry-level tasks in software and teaching carry low single-error stakes ($S \ge 2$) despite high learning value ($N_5 \le 1$).
- **Spot Verification Audit ($n=45$ Stratified Subsample):** Independent second-pass verification across $45$ randomly sampled items ($15$ per taxonomy) achieves **$100.0\%$ gate agreement**, **$95.9\%$ within-$\pm 1$ dimension agreement** ($83.3\%$ exact), and index Pearson $r = 0.8994$ ($\text{MAE} = 0.2025$).

## Repository Structure

- `rubric.md` — Directionally consistent markdown version of the Skill Safety Index Rubric (`0` = Lowest Safety, `3` = Highest Safety).
- `pipeline.py` — Individual item scoring pipeline across Stanford TaskNet, O\*NET 31.0 Tasks, and O\*NET 31.0 Skills, plus stratified subsample spot verification.
- `analyze_and_plot.py` — Computes summary statistics overall and by industry, Cronbach's $\alpha$ and leave-one-out diagnostics, spot verification agreement tables, and kernel density plots.
- `skill_safety_results_brief.tex` & `skill_safety_results_brief.pdf` — 4-page LaTeX results brief with all tables and figures embedded.
- `figures/` — Overall and industry-paneled kernel density distribution plots (`.pdf` and `.png`).
- `output/` — Scored item datasets (`scored_items_all_taxonomies.csv`, `spot_verification_subsample.csv`), summary statistics (`summary_stats_*.csv`), leave-one-out reliability tables (`cronbach_loo_*.csv`), and `analysis_summary.json`.
- `data/` — Source taxonomy files from Stanford TaskNet (Nov 17, 2026 release) and O\*NET 31.0 (`task_statements.csv`, `essential_skills.csv`, `transferable_skills.csv`, `content_model_reference.csv`).
